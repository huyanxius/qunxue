# Classroom artwork

Two original images generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP without changing the composition. These assets belong to the classroom pages.

## civic-observation.webp

Prompt: Original sophisticated Renaissance-humanist oil and fresco editorial painting, wide 16:9 university social research course cover. A quiet Renaissance town square seen from a stone balcony, small groups of ordinary people interacting near a fountain, two attentive scholars with notebooks observing discreetly in foreground. Theme: social observation and empirical inquiry. Restrained muted olive, limestone, umber, dusty blue palette; graceful linear perspective, soft daylight, tactile aged plaster, refined museum quality. No text, no logo, no watermark, no machines, no gears, no CGI, no copying an existing famous painting. Compose a completely new humane scene.

## humanist-seminar.webp

Prompt: Original Renaissance humanist oil painting for a premium humanities classroom banner, horizontal 16:9. Three scholars, men and a woman, gathered around an open folio at a walnut table in a serene stone loggia. Courtyard garden beyond arches, humane gestures of discussion, graceful architecture and linear perspective, tactile muted fresco surfaces, soft natural daylight. Refined limestone, olive, umber and faded ultramarine palette, restrained museum editorial quality. Entirely new composition. No text, logos, watermark, gears, machines, or CGI.
